Sheet music + santoor tablature
Each note is synchronized directly to the ABC sheet music. HIGH, MIDDLE and BASS show the register; the number is the course number, with 1 as the lowest and 9 as the highest course in that register. Chords show multiple course numbers at the same position.
Šur santoor layout

27-course layout: B1–B9, M1–M9, H1–H9. Numbering rises with pitch within each register.
